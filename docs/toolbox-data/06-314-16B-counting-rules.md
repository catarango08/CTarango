# 314.16(B)(1)–(5): Box fill counting rules (summary)
Use the Table 314.16(B) volume per conductor for each item.

1. **(B)(1) Conductors.** Each conductor that starts outside the box and terminates or is spliced inside counts as 1. A conductor that passes through without a splice or termination counts as 1. Conductors that never leave the box, such as pigtails and jumpers, count as 0.
   - An unbroken loop or coil at least twice the 300.14 free-conductor length counts as 2.
   - Exception: an EGC, or up to 4 fixture wires smaller than 14 AWG, that come from a domed luminaire or similar canopy into the box are not counted.
2. **(B)(2) Clamps.** One or more internal cable clamps count as 1, based on the largest conductor in the box.
3. **(B)(3) Support fittings.** Each type of fixture stud or hickey counts as 1, based on the largest conductor in the box.
4. **(B)(4) Device or equipment.** Each yoke or strap counts as 2, based on the largest conductor connected to that device. A device or equipment wider than a single 2 in. device box counts as 2 for each gang it needs.
5. **(B)(5) Equipment grounding conductors.** All EGCs together count as 1, based on the largest EGC. If 250.146(D) isolated EGCs are present, they add 1 more (1/4 allowance), based on the largest of them.

**Sources:** NEC 314.16(B) text via UpCodes/IRC E3905.12.2; NECA-IBEW reprint.

**UNVERIFIED (edition timing):** the "loop counts as 2" sentence, the over-2-in. device rule, and the separate isolated-EGC allowance were all added during the 2011–2017 cycle. It was not confirmed which of these appear in the 2011 text. Treat them as present in 2017 and 2023 only.
