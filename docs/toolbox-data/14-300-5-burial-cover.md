# Table 300.5: Minimum cover (inches)
2023 heading: "0 to 1000 V". 2011 heading: "0 to 600 V". See the 2023 numbering note at the bottom.

| Location | Col 1 Direct burial | Col 2 RMC/IMC | Col 3 Nonmetallic raceway listed for direct burial / other approved raceways | Col 4 Residential branch ≤120 V, GFCI, ≤20 A | Col 5 Irrigation/landscape ≤30 V, UF or identified |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All locations not specified below | 24 | 6 | 18 | 12 | 6 |
| Trench below 2 in. concrete or equivalent | 18 | 6 | 12 | 6 | 6 |
| Under a building | 0 (in raceway, or Type MC/MI identified for direct burial) | 0 | 0 | 0 (in raceway or MC/MI) | 0 (in raceway or MC/MI) |
| Under min. 4 in. exterior concrete slab, no vehicular traffic, slab extending ≥6 in. beyond | 18 | 4 | 4 | 6 direct burial / 4 in raceway | 6 direct burial / 4 in raceway |
| Under streets, highways, roads, alleys, driveways, parking lots | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 |
| One- and two-family dwelling driveways and outdoor parking (dwelling use only) | 18 | 18 | 18 | 12 | 18 |
| In or under airport runways, including adjacent no-trespass areas | 18 | 18 | 18 | 18 | 18 |

Notes: cover is measured from the top of the cable or raceway to the finished grade. Lesser depths are permitted at risers and terminations. Where rock is encountered, use a raceway with a 2 in. concrete cover down to the rock. Where a Col 1–3 method is used for a Col 4/5 circuit, the shallowest depth applies.

## Edition differences
- **2011:** heading reads "0 to 600 Volts". There are no low-voltage lighting footnotes on Column 5.
- **2017:** heading changed to "0 to 1000 V". Footnotes a/b were added to Column 5 "all locations" (6 in.), permitting lesser depth for listed low-voltage lighting systems (411) and pool/spa low-voltage lighting.
- **2023:** EMT added to Column 3 (EMT is treated like PVC/RTRC, e.g. 18 in. general). Note 6 added (direct-buried EMT must meet 358.10).
