# 220.82: Optional dwelling unit calculation
Applies to a single dwelling with a 120/240 V or 208Y/120 V service or feeder of 100 A or more.

## (B) General loads
1. 3 VA per sq ft of floor area (outside dimensions; excludes open porches, garages and unused spaces).
2. 1500 VA for each 20 A small-appliance branch circuit and for each laundry circuit.
3. Nameplate rating of all fixed appliances: ranges, ovens, cooktops, dryers not connected to the laundry circuit, water heaters, and permanently connected or fixed motors.
4. Nameplate VA of motors and low-power-factor loads.

The total of (B) is demand-factored as follows: **first 10 kVA at 100%, remainder at 40%.**

## (C) Heating and air-conditioning
Use the **largest** of the following:
1. 100% of AC/cooling nameplate.
2. 100% of heat pump nameplate when the heat pump is used without supplemental electric heat.
3. 100% of heat pump compressor nameplate plus 65% of central supplemental electric heat. If the compressor is prevented from running at the same time as the supplemental heat, the compressor need not be added.
4. 65% of electric space heating, if there are fewer than 4 separately controlled units.
5. 40% of electric space heating, if there are 4 or more separately controlled units.
6. 100% of electric thermal storage and other heating where the usual load is expected to be continuous at full nameplate.

The values are the same in 2011, 2017 and 2023.
