# 240.4(D) Small-conductor OCPD limits
| Conductor | Max OCPD |
|---|---|
| 18 AWG Cu | 7 A (with the 240.4(D)(1) conditions) |
| 16 AWG Cu | 10 A (with the 240.4(D)(2) conditions) |
| 14 AWG Cu | 15 A |
| 12 AWG Al/CCA | 15 A |
| 12 AWG Cu | 20 A |
| 10 AWG Al/CCA | 25 A |
| 10 AWG Cu | 30 A |

These limits apply after ambient and bundling corrections, unless 240.4(E) or (G) permits otherwise.

The values are the same in 2011, 2017 and 2023. The 18 and 16 AWG provisions have been in the Code since 2008.

**Sources:** EEPower, "NEC Basics: Overcurrent Protection Part 2"; EC&M "Stumped by the Code"; Mike Holt 240.4 excerpt (search synthesis of these sources).
**UNVERIFIED:** the exact conditions in (D)(1)/(D)(2), such as the 7 A and 10 A conditions and the listed fuse/breaker types, were not checked word for word against each edition.
