# 430.22: Single-motor branch-circuit conductors
- Minimum conductor ampacity is **125% of the motor FLC**. Take FLC from Tables 430.247–430.250 (430.6(A)(1)), not from the nameplate.
- Example: 10 HP, 460 V, 3-phase: 14 A × 1.25 = 17.5 A minimum.
- Exceptions: 430.22(E) uses Table 430.22(E) percentages for non-continuous duty, and there are special rules for DC rectifier-supplied motors and wye-start/delta-run.
- Several motors (430.24): 125% of the largest motor FLC, plus the sum of the other motor FLCs, plus other loads.

The same in 2011, 2017 and 2023.

**Sources:** Eaton Bussmann motor circuit notes (430.6 basis). EC&M "NEC Requirements for Motors". **UNVERIFIED:** the wording of the subsection letters in each edition.
